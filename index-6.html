Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This policy explains how the Vidorax app handles information when you use it.

1. We do not collect your data

Vidorax does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. The app has no account system, no sign in, no analytics and no tracking. We do not see your name, phone number, contacts, downloads or anything else you do in the app.

2. What the app does on your device

  • Clipboard: when you open the app it checks your clipboard for a copied reel link so it can fill the input for you. This content stays on your device.
  • Link processing: when you request a reel, the app sends that public link to a fetch service to locate the publicly available media file. The service receives the link itself, not any information about you.
  • Saved files and history: everything you save, and your in-app history, stays on your device only. Removing the app removes it.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: needed to fetch the media you request.
  • Storage: needed so the files you download can be saved into your public Download folder where your gallery can see them. To show WhatsApp statuses, the app asks you once to choose your WhatsApp media folder using Android's own folder picker, and afterwards reads only the status files inside the folder you chose. The app does not read, change or share anything else on your device.

4. Advertising

The version of Vidorax released now does not display ads. If advertising is introduced in a future update, it will be served by Google AdMob, in which case Google and its partners may use the advertising ID of your device to show ads, and this policy will be updated accordingly. You can turn off interest based ads in your device settings under Settings, Google, Ads.

5. Responsibility for content

Vidorax is a tool. You are responsible for respecting copyright and the terms of the platforms you download from, and for saving only content you have the right to save.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
